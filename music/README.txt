MUSIC LIBRARY (picked automatically by the mood the AI director detects)
Put tracks into these folders:
  energetic/  - excited, confident, angry, surprised videos
  upbeat/     - happy, positive videos
  funny/      - comedy
  chill/      - calm, curious, informational
  dramatic/   - serious, storytime, reveals
  emotional/  - emotional, sad, heartfelt
Music is automatically lowered under your voice and faded in/out.
Free, copyright-safe sources: YouTube Audio Library (studio.youtube.com -> Audio library), pixabay.com/music.
Tip: for Instagram, trending audio added inside the Instagram app gets more reach than baked-in music.
